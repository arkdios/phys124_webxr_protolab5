What to do

  1. Two known weights (200 g each) pull the ring off centre.
  2. Pick up the spring scale and drag it around the table.
  3. Find the spot where the ring returns to centre.
  4. Record the Force and Angle below as your Case A result.

The spring scale can be picked up and dragged anywhere on the table: gravity and friction hold it wherever you let go. The pulleys and hanging weights stay fixed for Case A.
